# 17.0 — Validation Scope & Method

This preliminary validation looks at the brand from five different angles because no single search establishes brand availability:

- Federal trademark risk — similarity of marks plus relatedness of goods/services.

- State/business-name availability — whether the legal or assumed name can be filed in Michigan.

- Common-law and marketplace use — unregistered businesses can still create legal and practical conflicts.

- Digital availability — domains, search-result confusion and social identifiers.

- Brand architecture — whether the chosen shorthand and sub-brand system strengthens or weakens distinctiveness.

The USPTO specifically recommends a comprehensive clearance search across federal records, state databases and the internet. Similarity does not require identical wording; sound, appearance, meaning and overall commercial impression can matter, especially when the goods or services are related.

## What existing use does — and does not — mean

- Existing use can be relevant to common-law priority and continuity analysis.

- Longstanding use of TAB Massage & Fitness does not automatically grant exclusive rights to every future name containing TAB or TAB Wellness.

- The date, geographic scope, goods/services and exact manner of use matter in a legal priority analysis.

- A trademark attorney should evaluate whether prior TAB use strengthens the position relative to later TAB Wellness users.

# 17.2 — Existing “TAB Wellness” Collision

This is the most important issue found in the preliminary scan. An active business publicly identifies itself as “TAB Wellness” and markets natural wellness products. A second live site identifies “TAB WELLNESS INC” and offers wellness products, memberships/content and wellness-related services.

| **Observed use** | **What was found** | **Why it matters** |
|----|----|----|
| TAB Wellness — tabwellness.net | Natural/preventative wellness products including sea moss, oils, herbs and botanicals. Site identifies itself as TAB Wellness. | Same broad wellness marketplace even though core offering is products rather than TAB’s service model. |
| TAB Wellness / TAB WELLNESS INC — tabwellness.online | Wellness products plus plans, booking, wellness service content, classes/community features and member areas. | Closer marketplace overlap because the site combines wellness products with service/community concepts. |

The presence of these uses does not automatically prohibit TAB Wellness Collective. Trademark conflict turns on a fact-specific likelihood-of-confusion analysis. However, adding the descriptive word “Collective” may not by itself eliminate concern if “TAB Wellness” is viewed as the dominant commercial impression.

> This is why “TAB Wellness Collective” may still be viable while “TAB Wellness” should not become the public shorthand.

# 17.3 — Federal Trademark Clearance

The preliminary screening did not surface an obvious exact-match federal mark for TAB Wellness Collective. That is encouraging, but it is not equivalent to a formal USPTO clearance search.

## USPTO conflict standard

The USPTO explains that marks can be confusingly similar based on sound, appearance, meaning or commercial impression. Goods and services do not have to be identical; relatedness can arise when consumers may expect them to come from the same source or when they travel through similar channels of trade.

## Formal search should include

- Exact wording: TAB WELLNESS COLLECTIVE.

- Dominant wording: TAB WELLNESS and TAB.

- Spelling, phonetic and punctuation variants.

- Marks with similar commercial impressions in wellness, massage, training, coaching and adjacent services.

- Related goods such as wellness products, educational content and memberships where consumers might perceive common source.

- Live federal applications/registrations, relevant state marks and common-law internet/business use.

- Owner, filing-date and first-use details for any potentially conflicting mark.

# 17.4 — What TAB Should Consider Protecting

## Priority 1 — TAB WELLNESS COLLECTIVE word mark

If counsel clears the name, the first protection discussion should center on the words TAB WELLNESS COLLECTIVE in standard characters. A word mark can protect the wording independent of a single logo treatment, subject to the registered goods/services.

## Priority 2 — Distinctive TAB logo/monogram

Once the visual identity is finalized, a sufficiently distinctive design mark can be considered separately. This should follow the name clearance rather than precede it.

## Priority 3 — Transform. Align. Become.

The philosophy should continue to be used, but it should not be assumed to have the same protectability or strategic value as the primary brand name. Similar “align / become / transform” coaching and wellness language is conceptually common, so counsel should evaluate distinctiveness before a separate filing decision.

## Likely service coverage to discuss with counsel

- Massage and bodywork / wellness services.

- Personal fitness and strength-training services.

- Wellness coaching and educational services.

- Life coaching / personal development services.

- Digital programs, educational resources or memberships if they become material offerings.

The correct trademark filing strategy depends on the exact services actually offered and planned. International classes should not be selected simply to accumulate protection; the identification of goods/services should match real commercial use and strategy.

# 17.5 — Michigan Business-Name Validation

Michigan launched the MiBusiness Registry Portal in 2025. The official Corporations Division now provides live Business Entity Search, Marks & Insignia Search and Name Reservation services.

## Required Michigan actions

1.  Run the exact legal/assumed-name search for TAB Wellness Collective in the live Michigan Business Entity Search.

2.  Review close variants, not only exact matches.

3.  Determine whether the business will rename the existing entity, file an assumed name/DBA, or use another legal structure.

4.  Reserve/file the name once trademark counsel is comfortable with the brand direction.

5.  If useful, search Michigan Marks & Insignia records as an additional state-level screen.

State entity-name acceptance and trademark clearance answer different questions. A Michigan filing can be allowed even when a separate trademark issue exists, so both checks are necessary.
