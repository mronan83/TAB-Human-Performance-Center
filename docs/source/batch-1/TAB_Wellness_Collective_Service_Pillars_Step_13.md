Individualized strength and movement built around real-life capability.

|     |
|-----|

WHAT IT IS

Personalized strength training designed to help people become stronger, move with greater confidence, improve physical capability, and build a sustainable foundation for everyday life. Training is adapted to the individual’s current ability, experience, goals, lifestyle, and physical considerations rather than forcing everyone into the same program.

WHO IT’S FOR

People who want to:

> • Build strength and physical confidence
>
> • Improve functional movement and everyday capability
>
> • Begin strength training in a supportive, non-intimidating environment
>
> • Return to regular exercise after time away
>
> • Improve balance, stability, mobility, and coordination
>
> • Maintain strength and independence as they age
>
> • Support recreational, athletic, or outdoor activities
>
> • Develop healthier and more consistent movement habits
>
> • Improve resilience for the physical demands of work and everyday life
>
> • Progress safely following rehabilitation, injury, surgery, or other health changes when appropriately cleared
>
> • Move beyond exercise programs that have felt unsustainable, confusing, or poorly matched to their needs

WHAT CLIENTS MAY GAIN

> • Greater overall strength
>
> • Improved movement quality and physical confidence
>
> • Better balance, stability, coordination, and mobility
>
> • Increased capacity for everyday activities
>
> • Improved muscular endurance and physical resilience
>
> • Greater confidence using weights and strength-training equipment
>
> • A clearer understanding of how to train effectively
>
> • Sustainable exercise habits
>
> • Measurable progress based on individual goals
>

Progress may mean getting stronger, moving better, feeling more capable, maintaining independence, returning to an activity, or simply developing consistency.

PERSONAL STRENGTH TRAINING

# The TAB approach

| **Personal strength training at TAB starts with the person, not the workout.** |
|----|

TAB considers where someone is today, what they want their body to be capable of, what barriers they are experiencing, and what kind of training realistically fits their life.

Programs are individualized and progressively adapted as strength, confidence, movement quality, and goals change. Training may incorporate resistance training, functional movement, mobility, stability, balance, conditioning, and recovery based on what is appropriate for the individual.

The environment should feel supportive rather than intimidating, particularly for clients who may not identify as “gym people,” have had negative fitness experiences, are beginning later in life, or are returning to exercise after significant life or health changes.

TAB takes a broader view of training. Strength is not treated as an isolated fitness goal. It supports how people move, work, play, recover, age, and participate in the things that matter to them.

When an injury, surgery, medical condition, or other health circumstance affects training, services remain within the appropriate professional scope and may require clearance or collaboration with the client’s healthcare or rehabilitation providers.

CONNECTION TO TRANSFORM. ALIGN. BECOME.

| **TRANSFORM** | Create meaningful change in strength, movement, confidence, and physical capability through individualized, progressive training. |
|:--:|----|
| **ALIGN** | Build a training approach that aligns movement, goals, abilities, lifestyle, recovery, and the activities that matter most to the individual. |
| **BECOME** | Develop the strength, confidence, knowledge, and consistency to become more capable in everyday life and more confident in what your body can do. |

WOMEN’S WELLNESS COACHING

# The TAB approach

| **Women’s wellness coaching at TAB starts with the whole person—not a checklist of symptoms, habits, or expectations.** |
|----|

TAB looks at the client’s goals, responsibilities, routines, stress, movement, recovery, relationships, environment, and stage of life to understand what wellbeing realistically looks like for that individual.

Coaching is collaborative rather than prescriptive. The goal is not to tell someone exactly how they should live, but to help them identify what matters, understand what may be getting in the way, and create practical strategies that fit their real life.

TAB’s approach is mindful that women’s wellness is often affected by changing responsibilities and life stages. Needs may shift during pregnancy, postpartum recovery, perimenopause, menopause, caregiving years, career transitions, aging, and other significant changes.

Rather than treating those experiences as problems to “fix,” coaching helps clients adapt, understand their changing needs, and make informed choices about how they care for themselves.

Where health conditions, symptoms, medications, or medical concerns are involved, coaching remains within its appropriate scope. TAB can help clients organize goals, habits, questions, and observations, while diagnosis and treatment remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

HOLISTIC LIFE COACHING

# The TAB approach

| **Holistic life coaching at TAB starts by looking at the whole picture—not just the goal in front of you.** |
|----|

People rarely experience challenges in isolation. Work affects health. Stress affects relationships. Responsibilities affect routines. Habits affect energy. Physical wellbeing can influence confidence, motivation, and daily life.

TAB helps clients step back and understand how those pieces interact.

Coaching is collaborative, practical, and individualized. The goal is not to prescribe a perfect lifestyle or tell clients what their priorities should be. It is to help them clarify what matters, identify where things feel out of alignment, and build realistic steps toward meaningful change.

TAB’s approach should also recognize that progress is rarely linear. Priorities shift, circumstances change, and setbacks happen. Coaching should help clients build adaptability and self-awareness rather than dependence on a rigid plan.

Accountability is part of the process, but it should feel supportive rather than punitive. The emphasis is on learning what works, understanding what does not, and continuing to move forward intentionally.

When concerns fall into areas requiring licensed mental health, medical, financial, legal, or other professional care, coaching remains within appropriate scope and clients may be encouraged to work with the relevant qualified professional.

CONNECTION TO TRANSFORM. ALIGN. BECOME.

| **TRANSFORM** | Recognize patterns, assumptions, habits, or circumstances that are no longer serving you and create meaningful change. |
|:--:|----|
| **ALIGN** | Bring your choices, routines, priorities, relationships, and use of time into closer alignment with what matters most. |
| **BECOME** | Build the confidence, self-awareness, resilience, and consistency to continue growing into the life and person you want to create. |

SERVICE ARCHITECTURE

# How the four pillars work together

The four pillars are intentionally distinct, but they are not isolated. Together they express TAB Wellness Collective’s whole-person approach and create multiple entry points into the same broader wellness philosophy.

**Massage & Bodywork —** Supports comfort, recovery, mobility, restoration, and the physical experience of the body.

**Personal Strength Training —** Builds strength, movement confidence, resilience, and physical capability.

**Women’s Wellness Coaching —** Provides specialized support for women’s habits, changing needs, self-awareness, and wellbeing across life stages.

**Holistic Life Coaching —** Connects the broader life context—priorities, habits, relationships, responsibilities, mindset, and intentional change.

| **The services can stand alone, work together, and support different moments in the same client’s Transform → Align → Become journey.** |
|----|

BRAND CONSISTENCY RULE

Regardless of which service a client enters through, the TAB experience should remain personalized, whole-person, grounded, supportive, credible, practical, and focused on sustainable change.
