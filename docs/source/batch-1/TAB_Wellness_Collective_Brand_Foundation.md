**HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT**

This document defines the stable strategic foundation for the rebrand. It should guide naming, messaging, service descriptions, website content, visual identity, logo design, social media, signage, and future program development. Creative work should be evaluated against these principles rather than developed independently from them.

**01 Target Audience**

*Who TAB Wellness Collective exists for*

|     |
|-----|

**APPROVED AUDIENCE STATEMENT**

| *TAB Wellness Collective is for people who want to improve how they feel, move, live, and grow through a personalized, whole-person approach to wellness.* |
|----|

**AUDIENCE DIRECTION**

The master brand is intentionally gender-neutral. It should be welcoming and relevant to a broad client base while allowing Women’s Wellness Coaching to operate as a specialized service pillar with its own targeted messaging.

**AUDIENCE NEED STATES**

- Relief, recovery, mobility, and better movement.

- Greater strength, capability, and physical confidence.

- Sustainable health and wellness habits.

- Personal growth and meaningful lifestyle change.

- Better balance across physical, mental, and everyday wellbeing.

**BRAND IMPLICATION**

TAB should not look or sound like a women-only brand, a conventional gym, or a massage-only practice. The master brand should be inclusive and whole-person; individual service pillars can become more specific where appropriate.

**02 Brand Promise**

*What every TAB client should consistently experience*

|     |
|-----|

**APPROVED BRAND PROMISE**

| *TAB Wellness Collective provides personalized, whole-person support that helps people feel better, move with confidence, build strength, and create sustainable change in their everyday lives.* |
|----|

**PROMISE PRINCIPLES**

- Personalized rather than one-size-fits-all.

- Whole-person rather than focused on one isolated goal or symptom.

**03 TAB Philosophy**

*Transform. Align. Become. as both journey and interconnected model*

|     |
|-----|

**TRANSFORM**

Create meaningful change in how you feel, move, think, and live.

**ALIGN**

Bring your body, habits, priorities, and overall wellbeing into better balance.

**BECOME**

Build the strength, confidence, and consistency to grow into the person you want to be.

**UNIFYING PHILOSOPHY**

| *Transform where you are. Align with what you need. Become who you want to be.* |
|----|

**HOW THE MODEL WORKS**

The philosophy should work in two ways at once:

- Client journey: Transform → Align → Become provides a simple sense of progression.

- Interconnected philosophy: Transform, Align, and Become overlap continuously; clients may be working in all three areas at the same time.

This dual interpretation should inform future program design, client experience, visual symbolism, and storytelling.

|     |

**NEXT PHASE**

**Visual Identity & Logo Exploration**

With the brand foundation established, visual development can now proceed against a defined set of guardrails rather than subjective aesthetics alone.

**VISUAL GUARDRAILS**

- Grounded — natural, stable, calm.

- Modern — clean typography, simple forms, uncluttered layouts.

- Strong — enough structure and visual weight to support the strength-training side.

- Warm — approachable rather than sterile.

- Holistic — subtle references to balance, integration, growth, and movement.

- Gender-neutral — not overly feminine, masculine, spa-like, or gym-like.

**COLOR / TYPE DIRECTION**

Favor earthy neutrals with one richer accent: warm off-white or cream, charcoal/deep graphite, muted sage or olive, clay/terracotta, deep forest, and potentially muted bronze or sand. Favor a clean modern sans-serif, optionally paired with a refined serif for warmth and sophistication.

**AVOID**

- Lotus flowers.

- Dumbbells.

- Massage hands.

- Excessive leaf motifs.

- Generic female silhouettes.

- Visual language that feels overly spiritual, clinical, spa-only, or gym-only.

**APPROVED LOGO CONCEPT TERRITORIES — EXPLORE ALL THREE**

**INTERCONNECTED TAB**

A clean symbol built from three overlapping or interlocking forms representing Transform, Align, and Become. Strongest tie to the philosophy and Venn-diagram concept.

**ROOTED GROWTH**

An abstract organic mark suggesting grounding, upward movement, strength, and evolution without relying on literal trees, leaves, or other wellness clichés.

**REFINED WORDMARK**

A typography-led identity where TAB is the hero and Wellness Collective supports it, potentially with subtle custom letterform or embedded-symbol details.

**DECISION HIERARCHY**

**A simple filter for future brand decisions**

**1.** Does it support a personalized, whole-person approach to wellness?

**2.** Does it feel grounded, empowering, personal, holistic, modern, and supportive?

**3.** Does it reinforce sustainable change rather than quick fixes or gimmicks?

**4.** Does it work across all four service pillars without narrowing the master brand?

**5.** Can it credibly connect to Transform. Align. Become.?

**6.** Does it sound like a trusted wellness partner—not a salesperson, guru, or medical institution?
