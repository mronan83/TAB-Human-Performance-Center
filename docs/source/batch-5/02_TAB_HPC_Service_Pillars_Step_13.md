TAB HUMAN PERFORMANCE CENTER
# Step 13 — Service Pillars
Five integrated disciplines supporting human performance for real life
Transform. Adapt. Balance.
Portfolio logic
The five pillars are not five separate businesses. Each is a different entry point into the same outcome: greater capacity, adaptability, agency and self-trust.
# 13.1 — Massage & Bodywork
## What it is
Personalized massage and bodywork that supports comfort, movement, recovery, relaxation, body awareness and physical adaptability.
## Who it is for
- Everyday tension, soreness and restricted movement
- Exercise and strength-training recovery
- Pregnancy/prenatal comfort
- Post-surgical support when appropriate and medically cleared
- Manual lymphatic drainage and swelling-support needs within scope
- Chronic-illness comfort/supportive care within scope
- People who need restoration, mobility support or a less reactive relationship with their body
## Potential gains
- Reduced muscular tension and physical stress
- Greater comfort and ease of movement
- Improved recovery support
- Better body awareness and confidence
- Support for circulation/lymphatic needs where appropriate
- A more useful recovery strategy rather than waiting for breakdown
## TAB role
Restore: Restore capacity so the body has more room to move, recover and respond.
## Transform / Adapt / Balance lens
TAB principle
Application
Transform
Address tension, restrictions and recovery needs.
Adapt
Improve the body’s ability to respond and recover.
Balance
Recalibrate effort, restoration and physical demand.
Restore capacity so the body has more room to move, recover and respond.

## Who it is for
- People new to strength training

- Adults focused on aging, independence and durability
- People building strength for work, recreation, parenting or outdoor life
- People seeking better balance, stability, mobility and confidence
- People returning after rehab, injury or surgery when appropriately cleared
## Potential gains
- Strength and movement confidence
- Stability, balance and coordination
- Improved everyday physical capacity
- Training literacy and self-efficacy
- Greater durability and confidence under load
- Sustainable movement habits

# 13.3 — Women’s Wellness
## What it is
Personalized coaching for women navigating changing needs, responsibilities, habits and life stages — without reducing the experience to symptoms or a prescribed version of wellness.
## Who it is for
- Women building sustainable routines around movement, nourishment and recovery
- Pregnancy and postpartum lifestyle support within scope
- Perimenopause and menopause lifestyle support
- Women navigating caregiving, stress, career shifts or major life transitions
- Women rebuilding self-trust and agency around their bodies and choices
## Potential gains
- Greater clarity and self-awareness
- Better adaptation to changing life stages
- More sustainable routines
- Improved self-advocacy and decision confidence
- Stronger alignment between wellbeing and real-life responsibilities
## TAB role
Adapt: Build the capacity to adapt as life and the body change.
## Transform / Adapt / Balance lens

# 13.4 — Integrated Nutrition
## What it is
Practical nutrition coaching that connects nourishment to energy, strength, recovery, stress, lifestyle, preferences and the realities of everyday life.
## Who it is for
- People who want to eat in a way that supports strength and recovery
- People overwhelmed by conflicting nutrition advice
- People rebuilding consistency after restrictive or all-or-nothing approaches
- People seeking practical meal structure, fueling habits and better energy
- Clients whose training, recovery and nutrition need to work together
## Potential gains
- More consistent nourishment
- Improved energy and recovery support
- Greater confidence with food decisions
- Practical meal and fueling routines
- Less all-or-nothing thinking
- Better integration with strength, recovery and life
## TAB role
Nourish: Nourish capacity without making food another full-time job.
## Transform / Adapt / Balance lens
TAB principle
Application

- People seeking greater agency and alignment

- More confident decisions
- Stronger boundaries
- Greater agency and follow-through
- Reduced overwhelm
- Better integration between values and daily life
