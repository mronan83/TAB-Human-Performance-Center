TAB HUMAN PERFORMANCE CENTER
# Step 19 — Rebrand Transition Plan
TAB Massage & Fitness → TAB Human Performance Center
Transform. Adapt. Balance.
Transition story
TAB Massage & Fitness is becoming TAB Human Performance Center — an evolution that better reflects the integrated work TAB now provides across bodywork, strength, women’s support, nutrition and life coaching.
# 19.1 — Transition Principles
- Evolve TAB rather than erase it.
- Preserve appointments, reviews, history, referrals, search equity and client trust.
- Do not launch the name publicly until clearance and domain/handle decisions are complete.
- Treat TAB Wellness Collective as an internal superseded concept if it never launched publicly; remove old draft assets so they do not leak into production.
- Make client continuity the primary success criterion.

# 19.3 — Transitional Messaging
Recommended public transition language:
TAB Massage & Fitness is becoming TAB Human Performance Center. The new name reflects what TAB has grown into: an integrated center helping people build strength, resilience, adaptability and capacity for real life.
For the first 90–180 days, use “formerly TAB Massage & Fitness” in the announcement bar, About/Our Story, launch email, social bio and select referral materials — but not inside the permanent logo.
# 19.4 — Website & Domain Migration
- Keep tabmassage.com indefinitely and use page-specific 301/308 redirects.
- Map every meaningful legacy URL to the most relevant new destination; do not redirect everything to the homepage.
- Update canonicals, XML sitemap, robots.txt, analytics and Search Console.
- Maintain old email aliases and legacy booking links during transition.
- Smoke-test the full mobile and desktop path from search → service → booking → confirmation.

# 19.6 — Google / Social / Directories
- Update physical/public branding first, then website, then Google Business Profile to reduce verification/confusion risk.
- Update Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook and relevant professional/referral directories from one canonical NAP record.
- Keep existing social accounts where possible to preserve followers and history.
- Pin a launch post explaining the evolution and the new five-pillar model.

# 19.8 — Staff / Referral Partner Readiness
- Every practitioner can explain why the name changed, what TAB means, and what 'human performance' means at TAB.
- Clarify that clients can still use one service; cross-service support is optional and goal-driven.
- Send referral partners a concise update, new logo, service architecture, referral instructions and new URL.
- Provide a one-page FAQ for front-line questions.
# 19.9 — Cutover Runbook
- Publish new website and verify SSL.
- Enable and test redirects.
- Verify Start Where You Are, contact and booking flows.
- Update Timely.
- Update Stripe/customer billing branding.
- Activate new email signatures and aliases.
- Update Google Business Profile and directories.
- Update social profiles and publish launch post.
- Change physical signage and in-studio materials.
- Submit sitemap / Search Console move and monitor.
- Run mobile and desktop smoke tests.
- Track confusion, failures and client questions daily during launch week.
# 19.10 — Success Criterion
A new visitor understands TAB Human Performance Center immediately, while an existing TAB Massage & Fitness client experiences the change as familiar, intentional and seamless.
