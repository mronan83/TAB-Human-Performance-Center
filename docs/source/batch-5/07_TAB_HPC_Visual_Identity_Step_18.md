# Step 18 — Visual Identity
A stronger, integrated performance identity that preserves warmth and humanity
Transform. Adapt. Balance.
Visual shift
The new identity moves away from botanical/spa-coded wellness cues and toward capability, integration, adaptability and motion — while keeping the grounded warmth that made the previous direction approachable.
Concept board for TAB Human Performance Center.
# 18.1 — Identity Concept
The primary symbol is a three-part interlocking performance mark. The three forms represent Transform, Adapt and Balance while the shared center represents the integrated human system. The mark should feel in motion without becoming athletic-logo aggressive.

# 18.2 — Logo System
- Primary horizontal lockup: symbol + TAB + Human Performance Center.
- Stacked lockup: symbol above TAB with descriptor beneath.
- Symbol-only mark for social avatars, favicon, app icon and stamps.
- One-color graphite version for practical print and embroidery.
- Reversed white/neutral version for dark fields and photography.
- The phrase Transform. Adapt. Balance. is a supporting philosophy line, not required in every lockup.

# 18.6 — Photography Direction
- Real people in real environments whenever possible.
- Show useful capability, recovery, nourishment, conversation and everyday movement.
- Natural light, grounded materials, tactile surfaces and restrained color.
- Avoid hyper-athletic intensity, biohacking labs, spa clichés, before/after aesthetics and generic 'wellness lifestyle' stock.
- Show men and women across a range of ages and life stages.
# 18.7 — Application Guidance
- Website: Bone/Linen fields, Graphite type, Deep Olive structure, Clay/Ochre used selectively for energy.
- My TAB: more restrained, functional UI with Montserrat and high-contrast accessible states.
- Signage: simple logo, high contrast, minimal supporting copy.
- App/social icon: symbol only.
- Programs/forms: simplified header lockup; avoid decorative branding that competes with usability.
