Edits made to the owner's originals (owner rulings, 2026-10-05):

- **Name:** the originals say "TAB Human Performance Practice" (HPP). The owner ruled the name is **TAB Human Performance
  Center** and that "Practice" is not used; every brand reference here says Center. The original `.docx` files are not
  committed because they carry the retired name.
- **Palette:** Step 18 §18.3 listed Deep Olive #4A5446, Clay #C66A48, Linen #E6E3D9 and Bone #F8F6F1. The palette file
  is final (Deep Olive #4A5A44, Clay #C46A4A, Linen #E8E3D9, Bone #FBF6F1), and §18.3 here is corrected to match.
- **Logo:** the approved artwork is the redraw of the board in `public/brand/` (sources and accuracy notes in
  `design/logo-redraw/`), with the board's spacing errors corrected and its "Aiign" caption spelled "Align".
